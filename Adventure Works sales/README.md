#  Adventure Works Sales Analytics Dashboard

##  Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** for **Adventure Works Cycles**, Microsoft's fictional bicycle manufacturing and retail company.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance, product demand, customer purchasing trends, and revenue distribution. It enables business stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), analyze product category performance, and support strategic decision-making through interactive visualizations.

---

#  Company Overview

**Adventure Works Cycles** is a global bicycle manufacturing and retail company that produces and sells premium bicycles and cycling products.

The company offers multiple bicycle categories, including:

- Mountain Bikes
- Road Bikes
- Touring Bikes
- BMX Bikes
- Hybrid Bikes
- E-Bikes
- Kids Bikes

Adventure Works serves customers across different regions and supports multiple payment methods, making it an ideal dataset for business intelligence and sales analytics.

---

#  Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer the following business questions:

- Which bicycle category generates the highest sales?
- Which product size contributes the most revenue?
- Which products have the highest order values?
- How are sales distributed across product categories?
- How do customer payment methods affect sales?
- Which products should receive greater inventory focus?

#  Business Insights

### Product Category Performance

- **Mountain Bikes** generated the highest share of total sales (**34.1%**).
- **Road Bikes** closely followed with **32.5%**, making these two categories the primary revenue drivers.
- **Touring Bikes** contributed **17.9%** of total sales.
- **E-Bikes** accounted for **11.2%**, while **Hybrid Bikes**, **BMX Bikes**, and **Kids Bikes** represented smaller portions of overall sales.

### Product Size Analysis

- Medium-sized (M) bicycles generated significantly higher order totals compared to Large (L) bicycles.
- This suggests stronger customer demand for medium-sized products.

### Product Performance

Among the highest-performing products were:

- AeroSpeed 1000
- DownhillDominator 1000
- CrossRider 1000
- Adventurer 1000

These products consistently generated strong order values.

### Customer Purchasing Behavior

The dashboard includes payment method filtering, enabling comparison of customer purchasing behavior between Credit Card and PayPal transactions.

### Interactive Analysis

Users can dynamically filter results by:

- Product Category
- Payment Method

allowing flexible exploration of sales performance.

#  Business Recommendations

- Maintain sufficient inventory for Mountain Bikes and Road Bikes to meet customer demand.
- Increase promotional efforts for Touring Bikes and E-Bikes to expand market share.
- Focus marketing campaigns on best-selling bicycle models.
- Analyze customer preferences for medium-sized bicycles to optimize future production.
- Use payment method insights to improve checkout experience and promotional offers.
