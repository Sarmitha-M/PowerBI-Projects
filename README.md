#  Tailwind Traders Executive Sales Dashboard

##  Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI Executive Sales Dashboard** developed for **Tailwind Traders**, a retail company specializing in home improvement and lifestyle products.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance by analyzing sales, revenue, profit margins, inventory, customer loyalty, and product performance. It enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics and make informed, data-driven decisions through interactive visualizations.

---

##  Business Objectives

The dashboard was created to answer the following business questions:

- How is the company performing overall?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which countries contribute the most to sales and profitability?
- How do sales and profit margins change over time?
- Which products sell the most units?
- How is customer loyalty distributed across regions?
- How much inventory is currently available?

## 📈 Key Insights

### Revenue Performance

- Modular Sofa Set generated the highest net revenue.
- Home furnishing and premium products contribute significantly to total revenue.

###  Country Performance

- USA contributes the highest yearly profit margin.
- UK and Australia are also major revenue contributors.
- UAE records the lowest contribution among the analyzed countries.

### Product Performance

- Floral Wallpaper, Porcelain Tiles and Premium Paint products recorded the highest quantity sold.
- Premium furniture products generate higher revenue despite lower sales volume.

###  Customer Loyalty

- UK and USA customers have the highest loyalty points.
- UAE has the lowest customer loyalty score, indicating an opportunity for customer engagement programs.

###  Profitability

- Profit margins remain relatively stable throughout the reporting period with several peak-performing periods.
- Revenue is concentrated among a few high-performing products.

###  Trend Analysis

- Revenue spikes indicate seasonal demand or promotional campaigns.
- Monitoring these periods can improve inventory planning and marketing strategies.

---

##  Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory for top-selling products to prevent stock shortages.
- Introduce loyalty rewards in low-performing countries to improve customer retention.
- Expand marketing efforts for high-margin product categories.
- Analyze factors behind seasonal revenue peaks and replicate successful campaigns.
- Focus promotional activities on underperforming regions to improve market penetration.
