#  Adventure Works Executive Summary Analytics Dashboard

##  Project Overview

This project showcases an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using **Microsoft Power BI** for **Adventure Works Cycles**, Microsoft's fictional bicycle manufacturing and retail company.

The dashboard consolidates **Sales**, **Orders**, and **Customer Analytics** into a single reporting solution, enabling business users to monitor operational performance, understand customer purchasing behavior, evaluate product demand, and support strategic decision-making.

The report consists of three interactive dashboard pages:

- Product Sales Report
- Order Performance Report
- Customer Analytics Report

Together, these dashboards provide a comprehensive view of business performance across multiple operational areas.

---

#  Company Overview

Adventure Works Cycles is a multinational bicycle manufacturing and retail company developed by Microsoft as a sample business dataset for analytics and reporting.

The company manufactures and sells:

- Mountain Bikes
- Road Bikes
- Touring Bikes
- BMX Bikes
- Hybrid Bikes
- E-Bikes
- Kids Bikes

Adventure Works serves customers across multiple countries through various sales channels and payment methods. The organization uses business intelligence solutions to monitor sales performance, customer behavior, inventory trends, and operational efficiency.

---

#  Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this dashboard are to:

- Monitor product sales performance.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Evaluate order processing performance.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Compare product categories.
- Track customer distribution across regions.
- Monitor order status and shipping methods.
- Support strategic business decisions through interactive reporting.
